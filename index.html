// About me

About.

I'm a sophomore at NYU Stern double-majoring in Computer Science and Finance, with a minor in Web Programming and Applications.

Before NYU, I was a competitive fencer representing the South Korean National Team — including a silver medal at the 2019 Hong Kong International Regional Circuit. I completed mandatory military service as a Social Service Agent Squad Leader.

During my service years, I built a YouTube channel to 30K+ subscribers and established partnerships with YouTube and Supercell. Since 2025, I've been working as a QA Software Engineer for Supercell, beta-testing and contributing to new game modes in Clash Royale.

In relation to my work with Supercell, I have a strong interest in video gaming, reaching a personal best of #1 in the world in Clash Royale.
