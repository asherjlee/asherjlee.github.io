About me

The player

I'm a sophomore at NYU double-majoring in Computer Science (CAS) and Finance (Stern), with a minor in Web Programming and Applications.

Before NYU, I was a competitive fencer on the South Korean National Team, including a silver medal at the 2019 Hong Kong International Regional Circuit. I then completed mandatory military service as a Social Service Agent Squad Leader.

During my service years, I built a YouTube channel to 30K+ subscribers and established partnerships with YouTube and Supercell. Since 2025, I've worked as a QA Software Engineer for Supercell, beta-testing and contributing to new game modes in Clash Royale.

Gaming is more than work for me: my personal best is #1 in the world in Clash Royale.
